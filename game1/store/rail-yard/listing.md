## Store listing

- **App name** (30): Rail Yard
- **Short description** (80):
  Draw your own train track and watch the trains run. No words needed.
- **Full description** (4000):

  Draw a train set with your finger and watch it come alive.

  • Draw track anywhere: join it to make switches, loops and sidings.
  • Put down trains: steam and diesel engines, coaches, coal hoppers,
    tankers, log wagons, container flats and a caboose.
  • Press play: the trains run by themselves, slow down for the end of
    the line and for each other, and back out of dead ends.
  • Tap a switch to send a train another way. Tap a train to stop it.
  • Build your own trains: add and remove wagons, pick colours, set the
    speed, turn the engine around.
  • Rub wobbly track with the brush to smooth it out.
  • Save your layouts to play with later.

  The trains move like real ones: couplings stretch and bunch up, cars
  sway on the curves and the steam engines puff.

  Every button is a picture, so children who can't read yet can play
  on their own. No ads, no in-app purchases, no internet, and no data
  collected.
